(3) Poster format and preparation for the workshop’s poster sessions 
Authors will present the paper as a physical poster at the workshop’s poster sessions. Authors should prepare a poster PDF with the appropriate format, print the poster, and bring a physical poster to the workshop. We will also make poster PDFs available from the workshop website next to the paper PDFs. 
There is no specific LaTeX template for preparing the poster PDF. Importantly, the following instructions must be respected for posters across all the ICML’26 workshops:
Make your posters in the portrait format with following dimensions: 
Max Height = 36 inches (91 cm)
Max Width = 24 inches (61 cm)
Note that this is different from the main conference size posters.  
Authors are responsible for printing their posters and can use any suitable services for printing. For instance, authors could print a poster at their home institute and transport it with them. Alternatively, authors can explore printing options at the venue provided at https://icml.cc/Conferences/2026/PosterInstructions (see section “Poster Printing”; order deadline is June 15, 2026).
By 22 June 2026 (AoE time), we request authors to share their poster PDF files. Please follow these instructions to share the poster PDF file:
Name the file as {ID}_poster.pdf where {ID} is a placeholder for your paper number in Openreview (e.g., 1_poster.pdf, 2_poster.pdf). Share the file by emailing <contact.genaicreativity@gmail.com> with “{ID} files” as the subject.
